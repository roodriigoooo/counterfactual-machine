HOW TO PLAY

Change the bounce.

  1. Read the clearing.

    Gold means pass through it. Red means stay out. The ball’s center decides whether it counts.

  2. Give the bumper a nudge.

    Drag the coral circle left or right, or use its slider and arrow buttons. It can move by one ball radius in either direction, before the ball drops.

  3. Drop the ball.

    Watch two seconds of actual physics. Replay repeats that same drop; moving the bumper lets you try a new one.

  4. Ask for an idea.

    A hint moves the bumper and shows a dashed, imagined path. Drop the ball to see if it really works. Hints can be wrong.

Keyboard: Tab to the bumper slider, then use ← / →. Space on a focused button activates it. Escape closes a menu.

How the hint chooses a move

The model predicts 257 possible bumper positions and chooses the smallest move with a predicted goal margin of at least 3 cm. Dropping the ball checks that single choice in the simulator. No suggestion does not prove that a solution is impossible.
